# Financial SRS guidance — project-authored engineering notes

Use the requested IEEE 830-aligned introduction, overall description and specific requirements sections, preserving requirement sources, acceptance criteria and verification. Cover user, hardware, software and communications interfaces with explicit dispositions. Describe financial data entities, lineage, confidentiality, retention decisions and logical integrity constraints. State reliability, availability, security, maintainability and portability requirements or record unknowns and justified exclusions.

Distinguish a proposed requirement, a candidate regulatory mapping and an approved institution policy. A document header, citation, confidence score or filled section does not establish correctness. Every financial business rule, operational target and approval should be traceable to supplied evidence or an explicitly labelled assumption. Unknowns remain open for human review.
