The older financial material under `docs/ingested/finance/` stays outside retrieval. The active, separately allowlisted `docs/ingested/financial/` corpus contains project-authored engineering guidance only. It supplies no legal thresholds, prescribed retention periods or current-law certification. Reviewed financial policies are attached separately with their exact source/version IDs.

Default candidate controls distinguish finance from e-commerce and commercial banks, NBFCs, insurers and securities entities. Unknown entity or jurisdiction becomes a scope-review decision and blocks baseline adoption where unresolved. Payment-provider rules, other lender classes, UK/US rules, organisation policies and additional activity-specific obligations require appropriate reviewed custom packs. The `FIN-PERIMETER` legal decision explicitly covers the source set and exclusions; lexical matches never establish complete compliance.

The knowledge UI supports domain-scoped official sources and custom candidate controls. Source revisions invalidate project input revisions and require fresh analysis and independent review. Remote-law monitoring and determination of the complete legal perimeter remain reviewer responsibilities.
